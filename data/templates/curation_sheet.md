# 📝 Pujo Pathfinder — Manual Curation Sheet

> **Purpose:** Use this standardized template to catalog new pandals, themes, and food spots sourced from Instagram, Facebook, and local scouts.

---

## 📌 Submission Guidelines

1. **Exact Coordinates Required:** Do not guess. Open Google Maps, drop a pin on the exact entrance of the Pandal/Food spot, and copy the `lat, lng`.
2. **Verified Sources:** Every entry must have at least one valid social media URL backing the claim (e.g., an Instagram reel showing the pandal theme).
3. **Zone Mapping:** Must strictly be one of `NORTH`, `SOUTH`, `CENTRAL`, `EAST`, `WEST`.

---
